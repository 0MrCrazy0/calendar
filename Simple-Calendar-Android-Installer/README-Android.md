# Simple Calendar for Android: How to Install

Simple Calendar for Android is a single file: **`Simple-Calendar-Android-Installer.apk`**. It's not on the Play Store, so you install it straight from this download. It takes about 2 minutes. You can also get it from the project's **GitHub Releases** page (the **Releases** link on the main page). Download `Simple-Calendar-Android-Installer.apk` from the latest release (v39).

## Step 1: Download

1. On your Android phone, open this page in **Chrome**.
2. Tap **`Simple-Calendar-Android-Installer.apk`** to download it.
3. If Chrome warns *"This type of file can harm your device"*, tap **Download anyway**.

## Step 2: Allow the install

1. When the download finishes, tap **Open** (or find the file in your **Files** / **Downloads** app).
2. If Android says *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*, tap **Settings**.
3. Turn on **Allow from this source**, then go back.

## Step 3: Install

1. Tap **Install**.
2. If **Google Play Protect** shows a warning about an unknown app, tap **More details**, then **Install anyway**. (The app isn't on the Play Store yet. It's safe to install.)
3. Tap **Open** when it finishes.

## Step 4: Turn on alerts (important!)

On first launch the app will ask for a few permissions. **Say yes to all of them**, or alerts won't fire when the app is closed:

- **Notifications**: tap **Allow**.
- **Alarms & reminders**: turn it **on** for Simple Calendar.
- **Battery**: when asked to stop optimising battery, tap **Allow**.
- **Full-screen alarms** (Android 14 and newer): if the alarm doesn't show over the lock screen, go to **Settings** in the app and tap **Allow full-screen alarms**.

### Extra step for Samsung, Xiaomi, Oppo, Huawei and similar phones

These phones can block background alerts. Go to:

**Settings > Apps > Simple Calendar > Battery** and choose **Unrestricted** (or **No restrictions**).

## Test it

1. Create an event **2 minutes from now** with a reminder.
2. Close the app (swipe it away) and lock your phone.
3. You should get the alert, even with no internet.

## Alarm sounds (new in v39)

Reminders now ring like a real alarm clock, on your phone's **alarm volume**, and keep ringing until you tap **Stop** or **Snooze**. They ring even when the phone is on silent or vibrate. Pick from 5 sounds (Classic, Chime, Digital, Bells, Siren) in **Settings**, tap **Preview** to hear them, and set the volume and ring time. Each event can have its own sound. If you'd rather have a plain notification, change **Alert style** to **Notification**.

## Never miss an alert

- Alerts are saved on your phone, so they work offline with the app closed and the phone locked.
- Open the app **at least once a month**. Repeating events are booked 30 days ahead, and opening the app books the next ones.
- Keep **Do Not Disturb** off, or allow Simple Calendar through it.
- Alerts come back automatically after you restart your phone.

## Troubleshooting

- **Notification but no sound**: install v39 over the old version. Then check your phone's **alarm volume** (not the ringer volume) is turned up.
- **No alert came**: check the permissions in Step 4, and set Battery to **Unrestricted**.
- **"App not installed"**: delete any older copy of Simple Calendar first, then install again.
- **Can't find the file**: open the **Files** app and look in **Downloads**.

## Updating

Download the new `.apk` and install it over the old one. Your events stay. v39 installs straight over v38.

## Uninstall

Press and hold the Simple Calendar icon, then tap **Uninstall**.
